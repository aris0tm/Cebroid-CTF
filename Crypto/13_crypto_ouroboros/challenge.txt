Crypto Ouroboros

The Ouroboros encrypts its own tail.
The file has been transformed repeatedly:

    Base64 -> Hex -> XOR(0x5A) -> ROT13 -> AES-128-CBC   (one cycle)

...then again. And again. And again (10 times total, same fixed key/IV every cycle).

Everything can be reversed - nobody's going to decode this by hand though.
Write a script that automates the reverse process. Stop when you see the marker: CEBROID_FINAL

File: ouroboros.enc

Note: the AES key and IV are the same simple 16-byte ASCII strings on every cycle
(this is a pattern-recognition & automation puzzle, not a key-cracking one -
the key material is discoverable from context/organizer hints during the event).
