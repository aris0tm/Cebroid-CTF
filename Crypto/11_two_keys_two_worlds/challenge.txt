Two Keys, Two Worlds

Someone wanted the best of both worlds. So they encrypted the message with AES...
...then encrypted the AES key with RSA. Unfortunately, the files were separated.

Files: payload.enc (IV || AES-256-CBC ciphertext), key.enc (RSA-OAEP encrypted AES key),
       public.pem, clue.txt (contains the RSA private key, base64-encoded, hidden in a log entry)
