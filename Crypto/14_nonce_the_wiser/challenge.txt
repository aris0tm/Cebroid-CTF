Nonce the Wiser

Everything was encrypted using authenticated encryption. The cryptographer was very proud.
Unfortunately, they reused something that was supposed to be unique.

Nonce the wiser.

Files: ciphertext1, ciphertext2, tag1, tag2, nonce (raw bytes),
       known_plaintext2.txt (the plaintext of message 2 - a known-plaintext hint,
       since GCM reuse means XORing the two ciphertexts cancels the keystream)

Goal: recover the flag (message 1's plaintext) via keystream reuse: ct1 XOR ct2 = pt1 XOR pt2.
