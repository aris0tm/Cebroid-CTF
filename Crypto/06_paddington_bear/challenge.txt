Paddington Bear

Paddington has a secret message.
He's willing to decrypt anything you send him.
After every request, Paddington tells you whether the padding was valid.
Surely that tiny distinction can't be useful... Can it?

Files:
  ciphertext.txt - hex(IV || ciphertext) of the encrypted flag
  oracle.py       - run locally; tells you ONLY "Padding OK" / "Invalid padding"
                    for any hex blob you feed it. It does not know or print the key.

Goal: recover the flag using only the oracle's OK/Invalid responses (CBC padding oracle attack).
