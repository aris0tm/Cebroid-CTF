### People Are the Patch

**The breach was patched. The people weren't.**

You found **Ethan Vale**, an employee of **Nexora Dynamics**, listed in a recent devastating data breach.

Unfortunately for Nexora, its internal restructuring hasn't happened yet.

The information available to you:

* **Name:** Ethan Vale
* **Position:** Senior Systems Analyst
* **Department:** Infrastructure Operations
* **Previous Manager:** Marcus Bell
* **Internal Project:** Project Atlas
* **Office:** Chennai Operations Centre
* **Employee ID:** NX-1847
* **Office Contact:** +91 xxxxx xxxxx

Your objective is simple:

Convince Ethan that you are an authorized member of the internal IT team and obtain the **CTF verification phrase**.

You aren't exploiting a server.

You're exploiting **trust**.

**Flag format:** `CEBROID{...}`

### Scoring

This challenge starts at **100 points**.

The score decays based on the quality of your interaction:

* **90–100% convincing:** 100 points
* **80–89%:** 80 points
* **70–79%:** 70 points
* **60–69%:** 60 points
* **50–59%:** 50 points
* **Below 50%:** 0 points

The organizer's evaluator determines the final score.

**Hint:** Authority is convincing. Context is convincing. Asking for a password immediately is neither.

> **Sometimes the weakest link isn't the network — it's the person holding the keyboard.**
