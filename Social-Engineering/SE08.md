# SE08 — Dead Drop

**Category:** Social Engineering  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Points:** 250

---

## Description

Nothing here belongs to you.

At least, that's what you're supposed to think.

Look around.

There are things people leave behind every day:

Notes.

Badges.

Business cards.

Schedules.

Documents.

Most of them mean nothing.

Some of them mean **everything**.

Your objective is to gather the information hidden in plain sight and uncover what happened.

**The environment is part of the challenge.**

**Flag format:** `cebroid{...}`

---

## Hint

> One man's trash is another hacker's intelligence.

---

## Organizer Notes

Scatter fictional information around the CTF venue.

Possible items:

- Discarded meeting notes
- Business card
- Visitor badge
- Printed calendar
- Sticky note
- Fake invoice
- Organizational chart
- Coffee-shop receipt
- Conference schedule
- Shipping label
- Abandoned document

Each item should contain only a small piece of information.
