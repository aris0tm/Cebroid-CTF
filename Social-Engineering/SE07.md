# SE06 — Wrong Number

**Category:** Social Engineering  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Points:** 100

---

## Description

Wrong number.

Wrong person.

Wrong conversation.

Right information.

Sometimes nobody needs to break into an account.

Sometimes the information is simply **given to the wrong person**.

Examine the conversation.

Find what shouldn't have been disclosed.

**Flag format:** `cebroid{...}`

---

## Hint

> They weren't supposed to tell you.

---

## Organizer Notes

Provide the player with a fictional message thread between employees.

The conversation should initially appear completely ordinary.

The target accidentally assumes the recipient is someone else and gradually reveals information.

Example:

> **Maya:** Hey Daniel, are you joining the Atlas meeting?
>
> **Unknown:** Sorry, wrong number.
>
> **Maya:** Oh! Sorry about that.
>
> **Maya:** Actually, since I have you here, do you know whether the new access code is still `ORBIT-17`?
>
> **Unknown:** ...
>
> **Maya:** Wait. You're not Daniel, are you?

The disclosed information leads to the flag.

The challenge should reward noticing **what was accidentally revealed**, rather than simply finding a suspicious message.

> **Wrong number. Right information.**
